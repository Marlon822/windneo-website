2011 — The Beginning

A Watchmaker's Obsession

WindNeo was born in a small workshop in Shanghai, where our founder — a lifelong horologist and collector — could find no winder worthy of his Patek Philippe collection. Every product on the market was either technically adequate but aesthetically vapid, or beautiful but mechanically unreliable.

The decision was simple: build one himself. The first WindNeo prototype took eleven months and three failed designs before the fourth version earned a permanent place on his desk — and the admiration of every collector who visited.

2015 — The Standard

Swiss Precision Meets Italian Artistry

By 2015, WindNeo had established partnerships with two Swiss motor manufacturers whose movements power some of the world's finest clocks — and with an Italian atelier in Florence whose leather craftsmen had spent decades working for the great luggage houses.

The combination — Swiss mechanical precision inside, Italian leather artistry outside — became the WindNeo signature. It remains so today: every product, from the Classic to the Imperial, is built to the same uncompromising standard.

2020 — The World

From One Workshop to 21 Countries

Word travels in the collector community. By 2020, WindNeo was serving collectors in 21 countries — from private residences in London and Monaco to boutiques in Tokyo and Geneva. The Bespoke programme, launched in 2019, had already produced commissions for watch clubs, luxury hotels, and private estates.

Through it all, we have kept one policy absolute: no piece leaves our atelier until the craftsman responsible for it is willing to put his name on the maker's plate. Every WindNeo carries that accountability — and that pride.

What We Stand For

Four principles, unchanged since 2011

01

No Compromise

Every component is specified to match or exceed the standards of the timepieces it houses. We do not make exceptions for cost or convenience.

02

One Craftsman

Each WindNeo is built by a single craftsman from first cut to final inspection. No assembly line. No handoff. One person, one responsibility.

03

Earned Exclusivity

We limit our Bespoke programme to 24 commissions per year — not as a marketing device, but because 25 would exceed what we can build with integrity.

04

Lifetime Responsibility

Every WindNeo comes with lifetime priority service. When your collection needs attention, so does the piece that holds it. We never walk away from our work.
