Crafted for the Discerning

The WindNeo Creativity series exists at the intersection of horology and contemporary art. Each edition is a collaboration with a different artist or designer, resulting in a piece that is as much a sculpture as it is a watch winder.

Every Creativity piece is produced in a strict edition of twelve. It carries an individually numbered certificate, the artist's signature, and a craftsman's plate, making it from the moment it leaves the atelier a collectible in its own right.
