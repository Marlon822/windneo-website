Crafted for the Discerning

Macassar ebony is among the rarest hardwoods on earth. Its dramatic black and amber grain is found in the finest concert grand pianos and the cabinets of heads of state. The WindNeo Royal is finished in the same material.

Each Royal cabinet is individually matched from a single ebony plank to ensure continuity of grain across every surface. The result is a piece that commands the room before it is even opened.
