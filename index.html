Bespoke Creation

Every WindNeo Bespoke piece is a singular commission — conceived, crafted, and delivered
for one collector alone. No two are ever the same.

Watch Clubs

Private members' clubs and horological societies commissioning a house piece — a single winder safe that embodies the club's identity and legacy.

Boutique Watch Retailers

Flagship boutiques and authorized dealers seeking an exclusive display and storage solution that reinforces their brand at the highest level.

Private Collectors

The connoisseur who has spent decades curating a Rolex or Patek Philippe collection and demands a keeper worthy of every watch in it.

Ultra-High-Net-Worth Gifting

For those who give at a different altitude — board gifts, client appreciation, and milestone presents that arrive already legendary.

The Bespoke Journey

From first conversation to finished masterpiece — typically 8 to 14 weeks.

01

Private Consultation

A dedicated advisor learns about your collection, your space, and your vision. Nothing is assumed — everything is listened to.

02

Design Proposal

Our atelier translates your brief into hand-drawn technical sketches and material swatches — delivered within two weeks for your review.

03

Material Selection

Choose from 60+ curated leathers, exotic veneers, precious metals, and silk linings — or supply your own material for us to work with.

04

Handcrafted Execution

A single master craftsman is assigned to your commission from start to finish — each component hand-fitted, each seam hand-stitched.

05

White Glove Delivery

Your piece arrives in a bespoke presentation chest with provenance certificate, craftsman's signature, and your commission number — the only one in existence.
